GuardGen

A lightweight tool for generating unique include guards for C/C++ header files.

Generate a UUID v7-based include guard, copy it, and paste it directly into your header file.

Unique

UUID v7-based guards help prevent header collisions.

Instant

Generate and copy a guard with a simple interaction.

Private

Runs entirely in-browser via WebAssembly.
