GuardGen

A lightweight tool for generating unique include guards for C/C++ header files.

Simply click to generate a new UUID-based guard and paste it into your code.

  • Unique Identifiers: UUID v7-based guards to prevent header collisions
  • Instant Generation: One click, ready to copy
  • Privacy: Runs entirely in-browser via WebAssembly. Generated data is not sent to any server.
